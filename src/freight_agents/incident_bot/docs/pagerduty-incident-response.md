Plain-language summary of the published guide.

## Severity levels

SEV-1 is a critical issue that warrants public notification and liaison with executive teams. SEV-2 is a critical system issue actively impacting many customers. Anything above SEV-3 is a major incident. SEV-3 covers stability or minor customer-impacting issues that need immediate attention from service owners. SEV-4 and SEV-5 are minor or cosmetic and handled as low-urgency pages or tickets. When unsure between two levels, treat the incident as the higher one.

## Roles

The incident commander leads and decides; a deputy supports the commander; a scribe records key information in the incident channel; subject matter experts investigate and act at the commander's direction; a customer liaison posts public-facing messages when the commander says; an internal liaison updates internal stakeholders and mobilizes responders.

## Update cadence

External updates go out at least every 20 minutes from the scoping update during the first two hours of a major incident, and less often afterwards. The first communication is decided within 5 minutes of starting the incident call. The internal liaison updates executives roughly every 30 minutes. The resolution notice goes out when the commander confirms full recovery.
